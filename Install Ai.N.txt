AMAZING INFO NEWS (Ai.N)
A daily generative-AI newspaper for your Mac's menu bar.

INSTALL (one minute)
1. Drag "Amazing Info News.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious
   software (it comes from a friend, not the App Store). Click "Done".
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Amazing Info News. Confirm with "Open".
   (You only do this once.)

USING IT
- Look for the newspaper icon at the top-right of your screen, in the menu bar.
- Click it for today's headlines. Use the arrows or the calendar to read
  earlier days. "Read the full edition" opens the whole newspaper.
- A new edition is printed every morning while your Mac is on. The first one
  is printed straight away.
- Settings… lets you open Ai.N automatically when you log in, pause daily
  delivery, or reprint today's edition.

OPTIONAL: CLAUDE EDITOR
If you have an Anthropic API key, paste it into Settings and Claude will edit
each edition: sharper headlines, "Why it matters" notes, By the numbers, and
a daily Pixel & Dez comic strip. It costs a few cents a day on your own
Anthropic account. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

TURNING IT OFF
Switch off "Daily delivery" in the menu, or choose "Quit Ai.N".
To remove it completely, drag the app from Applications to the Trash.

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
